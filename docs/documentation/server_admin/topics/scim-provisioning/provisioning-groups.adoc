[[_scim_provisioning_groups_]]
= Provisioning groups
[role="_abstract"]

By default, a destination provisions users only. When *Provision groups* (`supportsGroups`) is switched on, the groups in the
destination's scope are also created in the application, and their memberships are kept up to date.

* Every group listed in *Groups in scope* and each of its subgroups is created as a separate group in the application.
  Nested groups are not flattened.
* Each group carries its direct members that are enabled, referenced by their account IDs in the application.
* A group is never sent with a reference to an account that does not exist yet. It waits for its members to be provisioned,
  for a bounded number of attempts (see the `group-deferral-millis` and `max-group-deferrals`
  <<_scim_provisioning_node_options_,node options>>), and is then sent without the missing members. They are added when
  their accounts exist.
* A group that already exists in the application with the same name is adopted rather than duplicated.
* Renaming a group and changing its members are delivered to the application.
* Groups are never deleted in the application.

Deleting a group in {project_name} deprovisions those of its members who are no longer in scope by any other rule, one by
one, and leaves the group in the application unchanged. A mistaken group deletion in {project_name} cannot remove an
entire group, and its memberships, from the application.

*Everyone in the realm* does not provision any groups. Workflows act on users only, so groups are provisioned by change
capture, resynchronization, and reconciliation.
